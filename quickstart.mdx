This guide walks you through signing up for Samudra, connecting your first pump, and performing your first control action from the dashboard. By the end you will have a live pump visible on the Equipment Status card and know how to start and stop it safely.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A Samudra account (sign up at [app.samudra.io](https://app.samudra.io))
- A compatible pump controller unit with network connectivity
- The pump's serial number and network address (found on the unit's label)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your Samudra account">
    Go to [app.samudra.io](https://app.samudra.io) and click **Sign Up**. Enter your email address and choose a password. You will receive a verification email — click the link to activate your account.

    <Tip>
      If your organization already has a Samudra account, ask your administrator to send you an invitation link instead of creating a new account.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your first pump">
    After logging in, open the **Dashboard** and click **Add Pump** in the top-right corner. Fill in:

    - **Name** — a descriptive label (e.g., "Main Intake Pump 1")
    - **Serial Number** — printed on the pump controller unit
    - **IP Address or Hostname** — the unit's address on your local network
    - **Location** — optional, but helpful for multi-site deployments

    Click **Connect**. Samudra will verify the connection and show the pump in your dashboard within a few seconds.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check equipment status">
    Your new pump appears as an Equipment Status card on the dashboard. The card shows:

    - **Connection state** — green indicator when the unit is reachable
    - **Running state** — the large **PUMP ON / PUMP OFF** pill switch
    - **Live metrics** — flow rate, pressure, and temperature update every second

    If the pump is currently running, the switch shows an emerald track with the thumb on the right and the label **PUMP ON**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start or stop the pump">
    Tap or click the large pill switch on the Equipment Status card to toggle the pump between running and stopped states.

    - **Emerald track, thumb right → PUMP ON** (running)
    - **Grey track, thumb left → PUMP OFF** (stopped)

    The switch updates immediately to reflect the confirmed hardware state. If the command fails, a brief error message appears below the switch.

    <Warning>
      To immediately cut power to the pump in an emergency, tap the crimson **EMERGENCY STOP** button located beside the ON/OFF switch. Emergency Stop bypasses normal shutdown sequencing. See [Emergency Stop](/dashboard/emergency-stop) for details.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your first alert">
    Click the pump card's **Settings** icon (gear icon, top-right of the card) and select **Thresholds**. Set a maximum pressure value appropriate for your system. Samudra will send you a notification if that threshold is exceeded. See [Alerts](/monitoring/alerts) for more options.
  </Step>
</Steps>
